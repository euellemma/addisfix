# Brainstroming interventions, converging on a viable solution, and scoping;

Addis Fix: Structured Citizen-Sourced Incident Triage & Inter-Agency Mantainenance Tracker

The problem: slow-paced (long time of information flow between the citizens and the concerned body of solution), non-transparent, and unstructured/fragmented urban infrastructure problem reporting.

What is happening? We are solving lack of structured geo-tagged data/information reporting involving urban infrastructure defects.
What are these defects?
- potholes
- garbage accumulation
- blocked drainage
- damaged roads
- late or overlooked "to be built" infrastructure
- problems regarding water, electricity and other utilities
- accidents like fire, flooding and security issues

Why it matters: Of course, there are contemporary reporting mechanisms, but they lack structure, automation, severity scoring, transparency and accountability and slow information flow to the actual concerning body. This matters because the city agencies themselves rely on condition assessment, prioritization,
implementation and monitoring to manage infrastructure.

Focus point- we are focusing on trying to reduce the time gap between the problem and notice of the governing body by using different systems and solutions including awareness, access and web app development which in turn increase the pressure on the problem-solver party.
